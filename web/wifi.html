Connect to Wi‑Fi

You’re on the Adhan Player setup network. Choose your home Wi‑Fi so the speaker can join it.

Most phones open this page automatically (“Sign in to network”). If yours didn’t, open http://10.42.0.1:8080/wifi in the browser.

  1. Pick your home network (or type the name)
  2. Enter the Wi‑Fi password
  3. Tap Connect — then switch this phone back to home Wi‑Fi
  4. Open http://adhan.local:8080 — bookmark it (or Add to Home Screen on a phone)
