        Wi‑Fi
        
        
        If the Pi can’t join your home network, it starts a setup hotspot so you can configure Wi-Fi from your phone (no display or SSH needed).
        
        
        
        
        
        While the hotspot is on and nobody has joined it, the speaker retries your saved home Wi-Fi every few minutes (for example after a power cut, when the router boots slower than the Pi).
        
        
        
          Change Wi-Fi network
          
        
        
        
        LED cue on a Raspberry Pi: slow blink = waiting on setup hotspot; solid = online.
